What it's for

A private, friendly space for residents to share local news, swap advice and pass on things they no longer need. Every member is checked against a local address, so you know you're talking to neighbours.

How to join

  1. Tap Join on WhatsApp above, or scan the code below.
  2. Message the admin on WhatsApp with your house number, and your first name if you're happy to share it. Contact admin for verification
  3. Once you're verified, request to join the other groups and we'll approve you.

Please don't post these details in any group.

Buy / Sell / Donate

Pass on what you no longer need, or pick something up.

Group rules
  • Share a photo or two with a description and price.
  • Don't overshare photos or videos. Interested neighbours can message you privately for more.
  • Please don't post the same item twice.

Community rules

  1. Be kind and respectful. No insults, harassment or personal arguments. Take disagreements to private messages.
  2. Use the right group. Selling and giveaways go in Buy/Sell/Donate, general conversation goes in General Chat, and local advice, recommendations and common questions go in Important FAQs, Links & Findings.
  3. No politics, religious debates or offensive content.
  4. No spam or advertising. Local businesses run by residents can post once a month in General Chat.
  5. Respect privacy. Don't share other people's phone numbers, house numbers, or photos of people, children or number plates without consent.
  6. No naming or accusing people. If you see something suspicious, report it to the police rather than posting accusations here.
  7. This is not an emergency service. Call 999 in an emergency, or 101 for non-urgent police matters.
  8. Check before sharing. Please don't forward rumours, scam warnings or chain messages unless you know they're true.
  9. Keep it neighbours only. Don't share the invite link. New members must be verified by an admin.
  10. Moving away? Let an admin know and we'll remove you from the community.

Admins may remove messages or members who break these rules.

Your privacy

House numbers are sent privately to an admin and are never posted in the groups. We keep a simple list of verified addresses so we know members live here, and we remove people when they move away. Nothing is shared with anyone else.
